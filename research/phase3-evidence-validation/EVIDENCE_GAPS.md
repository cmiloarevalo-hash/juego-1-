## Experiment contract template
For every product-data experiment record:
- hypothesis;
- control/variant when causal comparison is required;
- event/metric;
- expected behavior;
- negative signal;
- GO criterion;
- MODIFY criterion;
- KILL criterion;
- data sufficiency statement based on stable/repeated behavior and uncertainty, not an invented sample size.

## Stop rule
Do not commission more desk research merely to increase confidence cosmetically. When a decision above is marked product-specific, next evidence should come from the APK, observed players, telemetry or a controlled product experiment. External research should resume only for a new factual/policy question or a materially different product alternative.
