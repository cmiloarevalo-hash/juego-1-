## Scenario hypotheses
**Village:** strongest onboarding affordance; simple streets/plaza; Holiday Kit-type props available as candidates.
**Snow forest:** route-width/visibility/obstacle variation; vegetation/overdraw/readability risks.
**Ice fortress:** natural climax/boss arena; shader/transparency/reflection and originality risks.
All remain PROPOSED; none is confirmed final.

## Minimum demonstrative content
A single complete authored stage can demonstrate the confirmed product if it includes:
- onboarding of lateral control;
- at least one meaningful gate choice;
- army growth/loss;
- one avoid interaction and one combat interaction;
- at least one obstacle/tool interaction if tools are approved;
- boss encounter (confirmed product requirement);
- victory/defeat/result;
- ES/EN critical HUD;
- enough repeated modules to test authoring/reuse.
Exact counts/length are intentionally not specified.

## Progression/difficulty
Metagame is OUT_OF_SCOPE. “Progression” here means within-run/ordered-stage teaching and challenge, not kingdom/economy.
Candidate ordering: teach one mechanic → combine two → pressure/readability → boss integration.
Difficulty values require playtest; no numeric curve invented.

## Gate/obstacle/combat distribution
Author by segment purpose, not fixed quota. Each segment declares teaching/decision/challenge/recovery/final role. Avoid stacking a new gate operation, new obstacle and new enemy simultaneously before comprehension is established.

## Recommendation
**RECOMMENDATION / PENDING HUMAN DECISION:** target **1 complete vertical-slice stage** as minimum implementation scope, architect the modular kit for 2–3, and treat a second stage as the strongest optional demonstration of reuse. Do not commit to three until art/performance/QA evidence exists.
If visual breadth is a primary demonstration goal, choose 2; if delivery risk is primary, choose 1. Three is not evidence-supported as minimum.

## Boss
Boss YES. Recommendation: final encounter of the last stage in the approved prototype scope. This does not decide whether that stage is village/forest/fortress or boss mechanics.
