## Implementation-ready visual grammar proposal
**PROPOSED baseline:** low-poly + toy-workshop/diorama language.
- shapes: large readable primary masses, limited micro-detail;
- Santa: broad anchor silhouette, original facial/proportion/costume detailing; not a direct rendition of a known media Santa;
- helpers: smaller repeated humanoid base with modular headwear/accessory/material variation;
- enemies: distinct mass families (round/heavy, tall/narrow, mechanical/blocky), not recolored helpers;
- boss: silhouette at least materially distinct in scale/mass/motion; original concept/model sheet required;
- materials: mostly opaque/matte; snow/ice highlights selective;
- gates: operation symbol/value + shape/border; never color-only;
- obstacles/tools: paired silhouette/icon affordances;
- VFX: short/localized hierarchy for gate, hit, death, tool, boss telegraph, result;
- HUD: army/status/result first; no baked ES/EN strings in art unless explicitly localized.
